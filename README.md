An unfinished CLI version of the card game named War.

The game IRL

Setup
* The Deck: Use a standard 52-card deck without jokers.
* The Deal: Shuffle the cards and deal 26 face-down cards to each player. Do not ever look at your cards. Keep them in a stack in front of you.

How to Play
* The Flip: Both players flip their top card face-up at the same time.
* Card Ranks: Aces are high. Cards rank from Ace down to 2 (Ace, King, Queen, Jack, 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2). Suits do not matter.
* Winning a Round: The player with the higher-ranking card takes both cards and places them face-down at the bottom of their stack. 

What Happens in a "War"
* If both players flip cards of the same rank (for example, two 7s), a War begins.
* Each player places three cards face-down on the table.
* Each player then places one new card face-up.
* The player with the higher new face-up card wins all 10 cards in the pile.
* If the new face-up cards tie again, another set of three face-down cards and one face-up card is played until someone wins.

Winning the Game
* The game continues until one player wins all 52 cards.
